# L1.0 — Product Vision & Personas

## Vision

One operator (scaling to a small team) runs many Fanvue models as if each had a full-time social agency behind it. AXIOM automates ideation, generation, compliance-checking, scheduling, cross-posting, community funneling, and analytics — while keeping the human in control of what actually ships, reachable from their phone. The system learns from its own results and gets better at going viral over time, and it runs on a single inexpensive box.

## Positioning vs v1

v1 was a strong enterprise blueprint but coupled to specific vendors (Vercel/Clerk/Fanlynks) and thin on non-X/IG platforms, on-the-go generation control, crash reporting, and a real learning loop. v2 keeps every capability, decouples the vendors, broadens to 10 networks, and adds the control/observability/learning subsystems.

## Personas

**P1 — The Operator (primary).** Solo owner running 1–20 models. Technical enough to configure proxies and connectors, but wants the day-to-day to be: glance at a phone card, tap approve, move on. Cares about cost, account safety, and not double-posting.

**P2 — The Chatter.** Handles DMs only, on assigned shifts, for assigned models. Never sees network config or billing.

**P3 — The Content Creator.** Manages assets and the scheduler for assigned models; can generate and stage but not publish without approval.

**P4 — The Model.** Sees only their own dashboard: their calendar, their analytics, their earnings, their fan CRM. No cross-model visibility.

**P5 — The Agent (non-human).** OpenClaw / Hermes / Zeroclaw or any MCP client operating under a permission tier (Viewer / Operator / Manager / Autonomous), scoped per model, on short-lived capability tokens.

## RBAC summary

| Role | Models | DMs | Assets/Scheduler | Publish | Network cfg | Billing/Org | Relay control |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Owner | all | yes | yes | yes | yes | yes | yes |
| Chatter | assigned | yes | no | no | no | no | DM cards only |
| Content Creator | assigned | no | yes | via approval | no | no | content cards |
| Model | own | own | own (view) | no | no | no | own cards (opt) |
| Agent:Viewer | scoped | read | read | no | no | no | n/a |
| Agent:Operator | scoped | reply | run generators, deploy links | pre-approved only | no | no | n/a |
| Agent:Manager | scoped | yes | create/modify | requires approval | no | no | n/a |
| Agent:Autonomous | scoped | yes | yes | yes | yes* | no | n/a |

\* Autonomous network-config changes are gated behind an explicit per-model grant and are dashboard-audited; they cannot be granted from the Relay (R-07).

Full role → capability enforcement is specified in `L3-specification/L3.0-api-and-mcp-contracts.md`.
