# L2.8 — Viral Memory Loop (Self-Improving)

## Requirement satisfied

Remember posts that went viral, learn what made them work, and continuously get better at generating winners. A closed loop: **generate → post → measure → label → embed → retrieve → bias → repeat.**

## 1. Performance ingestion (F-79)

After a post publishes, `metrics.poll` requests provider snapshots at 1h, 6h, 24h, 7d, 14d, 30d, 60d and 90d. Snapshots are written to the `post_metric` Timescale hypertable. Views, likes, shares, comments and engagement rate use shared columns; finite numeric dimensions declared by a connector (such as reach, saves, clicks or watch time) are preserved in `provider_metrics`. Missing or unsupported dimensions are not imputed.

The per-post analytics projection selects the latest matching provider snapshot and joins first-party short-link clicks and signed Fanvue subscription, PPV-purchase and refund events through the published target's stored post UTM identity. Link-click rate is tracked link clicks divided by provider views when views are positive. Revenue is net of attributed refunds and remains grouped by currency. The query does not claim unique visitors or external link-bio provider analytics, and this path does not depend on Timescale continuous aggregates.

## 2. Viral labeling (F-80)

Labeling is **dynamic and relative**, per platform and per model (a "viral" Reel for a 5k-follower model differs from a 500k one):
- Compute the post's percentile within the model×platform performance distribution over a trailing window.
- Cross a configurable percentile (e.g., ≥ p90 on the primary success metric for that platform — CTR-to-link and conversions weighted heavily since revenue is the goal) → label `high_performer`.
- Drift detection watches the distribution so thresholds track the model's growth (R-08).

## 3. Generative-recipe capture (F-81)

For each labeled post, snapshot the **full recipe** into `viral_recipes`:
`{ S3 shoot config (attire/environment/lighting/…), caption text + structure, hook (first line/thumbnail-text), hashtag set, posting time/day, format (Reel/Short/carousel/thread), platform, thumbnail feature descriptors, ToS score, realized metrics }`.
This is what makes success **replayable**, not just observable.

## 4. Embedding & index (F-82)

Embed the caption + hook (and optionally a vision embedding of the thumbnail) → store in `viral_embeddings` (pgvector, HNSW) keyed to `recipe_id`, filterable by model/platform. No external vector service; retrieval is a single SQL query.

## 5. Retrieval → generation bias (F-83)

At generation time, the Master Prompt Engine retrieves **top-K** recipes most similar to the intended theme for the target model+platform and injects them as **exemplars in S2** (TOKENKILLER-friendly; see `L2.5`). The generator is thereby biased toward patterns that demonstrably worked for *this* model on *this* platform — "here are 5 of your best-performing hooks/captions/formats; produce new work in that vein."

## 6. Explore/exploit (F-84)

A **contextual bandit** chooses among candidate arms — caption structure, verified hook/format and scheduled UTC bucket — per (model, platform, context). For a published post with a stored per-post short link, reward combines tracked clicks (25%) and net attributed Fanvue subscriptions/PPV purchases after refunds (75%), divided by the latest provider view count and clamped to [0, 1]. A post without a per-post link retains the existing relative provider-engagement reward. A verified fixed-post A/B winner promotes that post's reward to 1. Evidence decays with a 30-day half-life; weights live in `bandit_state`, with an exploration floor retained during arm selection.

## 7. Insight surfacing (F-85)

- A **"What's working"** dashboard panel: e.g., "Reels at 7–9pm with hook-style X convert 2.3× baseline (n=14)."
- The **calendar's optimal-time suggestions** (F-10) and **trigger-rule thresholds** (F-19) read from the same learned distributions.
- Optional weekly schedules queue a completed-week insight, persist a model-scoped Relay card and continue only while the same schedule remains enabled. External Relay delivery remains separately visible from the durable stored card.

## 8. Scope & privacy (F-86)

- Learning is **per-model by default** (persona-specific).
- **Org-level cross-model pattern sharing** is opt-in per model. A model must opt in to contribute or receive abstract patterns. Shared dashboard groups require at least five published examples across at least two other opted-in models, and only structural fields are returned; captions, assets, identities, source-model IDs, and post IDs are never shared. The lookup is same-org only, and RLS still applies to the underlying raw data.

## Loop integrity

- The learner only accepts published targets with a matching provider snapshot. A stored per-post link adds observed click and signed Fanvue conversion/refund outcomes; posts without one use relative provider engagement until a tracked link exists. Insights describe observed evidence and do not claim causation.
- Recency decay keeps it current with platform algorithm shifts.
- All learning artifacts are tenant-isolated (LBI-01) and never leak into another org's generation.

```
        │                                                              │
        └───────────── bandit updates arm weights ◄────── reward ◄─────┘
```
